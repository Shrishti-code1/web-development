🔬 Pre-Lab Sequence

Follow these steps before every practical class:

  1. Review the lab manual shared on GLA portal
  2. Complete pre-lab questions in your notebook
  3. Bring your laptop with Python/Jupyter installed
  4. Form groups of 3-4 for group experiments
  5. Submit pre-lab report to the instructor
  6. Download required datasets from GLA cloud

📖 Academic Terms Glossary

Lecture
Teacher-led instruction for a large group of students (50+ students)
Lab
Practical hands-on session in a computer lab with 25-30 students
Tutorial
Small group discussion and problem-solving session (15-20 students)
Internal Assessment
Continuous evaluation through assignments, quizzes, and tests (30% of final grade)
Practical
Applied examination testing hands-on coding and implementation skills (25% of final grade)
AI
Artificial Intelligence - the simulation of human intelligence in machines
ML
Machine Learning - subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data
