🎭 Theme: "Innovate, Create, Celebrate"

🎊 Welcome to the Annual College Fest!

Welcome to the most anticipated event of the year at GLA University, Greater Noida! Our annual college fest brings together students from all departments for three days of competitions, performances, innovation, and celebration. Join us as we showcase talent, foster community, and create memories that will last a lifetime!

What to expect: Technical competitions, cultural performances, gaming tournaments, food stalls, and much more! Over 500 students participate every year.

📝 Registration Process

Follow these steps carefully to register:

  1. Visit the registration desk near the main auditorium
  2. Fill out the registration form with your details
  3. Select the events you want to participate in
  4. Pay the registration fee (₹100 per event)
  5. Collect your participant ID card
  6. Attend the orientation session at 8:30 AM on Day 1
  7. Join the WhatsApp group for updates

📋 General Fest Rules

  • Mandatory: All participants must wear their ID cards
  • Mandatory: Reach the venue 30 minutes before your event
  • No outside food or drinks allowed in the venue
  • Respect all participants, judges, and coordinators
  • Follow the schedule strictly - no rescheduling requests
  • No unfair practices in competitions (disqualification)
  • Keep the campus clean - use dustbins
  • Photography allowed only in designated areas

📖 Event Definitions

Open Event
Anyone can participate without any restrictions - all students welcome!
Team Event
Participants compete in groups of 2-6 members depending on the event
Individual Event
Participants compete alone - only one person per entry
Registration
The process of signing up for an event (deadline: March 10, 2026)
Coordinator
The faculty member responsible for managing and judging an event
Final Round
The last competition stage to determine winners (top 5 qualify)
Wildcard Entry
Special entry allowed after registration deadline (extra fee applies)
