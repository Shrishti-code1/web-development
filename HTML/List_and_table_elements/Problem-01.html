Admission Overview

Welcome to GLA University, Greater Noida! We are excited to help you begin your academic journey. Our admission process is designed to find passionate and dedicated students who will contribute to our community and excel in their chosen fields.

Eligibility

To be eligible for admission at GLA University, you must meet the following basic requirements:

  • 10+2 (or equivalent) with minimum 60% marks
  • Valid score in GLAET (GLA Entrance Test) or JEE Mains
  • Mathematics and Physics as compulsory subjects
  • English proficiency (for international students)

Application Process

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Visit the official GLA University admission portal
  2. Fill the online application form with your details
  3. Pay the application fee of ₹1000
  4. Upload your academic documents and photograph
  5. Appear for GLAET or submit JEE Mains score
  6. Attend the counselling session (if shortlisted)
  7. Pay the admission fees and confirm your seat

Required Documents

Please gather these documents for admission:

  • 10th and 12th mark sheets
  • Transfer Certificate from previous school
  • Character Certificate
  • GLAET/JEE Mains scorecard
  • Passport size photographs (6 copies)
  • Aadhar Card copy
  • Medical Certificate
