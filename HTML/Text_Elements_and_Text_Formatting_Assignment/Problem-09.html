Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

All students must wear proper uniform.

Note: Uniform should be neat and clean.

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory.

If attendance is less than 75%, you will be debarred from exams.

Attendance will be taken twice a day
at 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM.

Examination

Exams will start from January 10, 2027.

Last date to submit exam forms is December 20, 2026.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted online.

Deadline: December 15, 2026
No assignments will be accepted after the deadline.

Discipline

Ragging is strictly prohibited.

Any student found involved in ragging will be expelled.
