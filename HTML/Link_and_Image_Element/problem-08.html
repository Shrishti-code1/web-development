GLA University Partners with Microsoft and Google Cloud

GLA University has become India's first Microsoft GenAI Campus and North India's first Google Cloud Agentic Campus . This partnership will bring cutting-edge AI technologies to students including Generative AI, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing .

Students will get direct access to Microsoft JN-I and Google Gemini Enterprise tools. This is a historic milestone for technical education in Uttar Pradesh.

GLA University Greater Noida Campus successfully hosted the First Global AI Summit 2026 in January. The summit was organized in collaboration with the Ministry of Electronics and IT .
