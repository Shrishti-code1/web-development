GLA University Greater Noida Campus

About

GLA University Greater Noida Campus is located in Knowledge Park II. The campus was established to provide industry-integrated education. It focuses on practical learning and real-world projects. The campus has modern infrastructure with smart classrooms and advanced labs.

Programs

We offer 84+ programs including B.Tech, BBA, BCA, B.Com (Hons), MBA and Integrated L.L.B. Our B.Tech programs are available in Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical and Civil Engineering. BBA has specializations like Family Business and Marketing.

Campus Life

The campus has modern classrooms, computer labs, library and sports facilities. Students participate in technical fests, cultural events and hackathons. Hostel facilities are available for both boys and girls. The campus also has a cafeteria and recreational areas.
