Agra Weekend Getaway Guide

Discover the city of love, history, and incredible food

Top Attractions

Agra is home to the world-famous Taj Mahal, one of the Seven Wonders of the World. Other must-visit places include Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri, and Mehtab Bagh.

Culture

Agra has a rich cultural heritage influenced by Mughal architecture and traditions. The city is famous for its marble inlay work, Zardozi embroidery, and Mughlai cuisine. Local festivals like Taj Mahotsav celebrate this vibrant culture.

Food

Agra is a food lover's paradise. Don't miss the famous Petha (sweet made from ash gourd), Mughlai biryani, and dalmoth. The street food near Sadar Bazaar is absolutely delicious.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is the best time to visit Agra. The weather is pleasant and perfect for sightseeing. Avoid visiting during April to June as temperatures can go above 45°C.

Travel Tips

Important Tip: Visit the Taj Mahal during sunrise for the most beautiful view. Book tickets online in advance to avoid long queues.
