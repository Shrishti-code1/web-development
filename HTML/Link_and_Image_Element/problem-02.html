GLA University Creates History with Microsoft-Google AI Campus

University becomes India's first Microsoft GenAI Campus and North India's first Google Cloud Agentic Campus

GLA University has achieved a historic milestone by partnering with global tech giants Microsoft and Google Cloud. The Mathura campus will now become India's first Microsoft GenAI Campus and North India's first Google Cloud Agentic Campus.

The announcement was made in June 2026, marking a new era for technical education in Uttar Pradesh. This partnership will bring cutting-edge AI technologies to the campus including Generative AI, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing.

Students will now get direct access to Microsoft JN-I and Google Gemini Enterprise tools. The university will also establish Centers of Excellence for AI research and innovation. Faculty and students will benefit from Google Workspace for Education and advanced cloud infrastructure.

This development comes after GLA University's Greater Noida campus successfully hosted the First Global AI Summit 2026 in January, in collaboration with the Ministry of Electronics and IT [citation:1]. The summit focused on "Impact of AI on People, Planet and Progress" and featured experts from India and the USA.

Editor's Note: This news has been verified from official sources including Dainik Bhaskar and Hindustan Times. The partnerships are expected to significantly boost placement opportunities for students.
