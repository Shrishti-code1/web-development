About the Institution

GLA University was established in 1992. The Greater Noida Campus was set up in Knowledge Park II to provide industry-integrated education. The campus focuses on practical learning and real-world projects. We have over 15,000 students and 800+ faculty members across both campuses.

The university offers 84+ programs in Engineering, Management, Law, and Humanities. Our student-centered approach combines academics with co-curricular activities, sports, and community service.

Principal / Director Message

Prof. Diwakar Bhardwaj is the Pro Vice-Chancellor of GLA University. He has over 25 years of experience in education and research. He believes that every student has unique potential and the university's role is to nurture it.

"Education is the most powerful weapon to change the world." - Prof. Diwakar Bhardwaj
