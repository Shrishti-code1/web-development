Travel Tips

  • Stay Hydrated: Carry water bottles at all times
  • Dress Modestly: Respect local customs and traditions
  • Bargain Wisely: Negotiate prices at local markets
  • Try Local Food: Street food is a must-try experience
  • Carry Cash: Many local vendors don’t accept cards
