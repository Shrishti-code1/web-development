Educational Topics

Savings Account Basics

A savings account lets you deposit money safely and earn interest. Interest rates usually range from 2.5% to 7% per annum. Minimum balance rules may apply.

Credit Cards & Responsible Use

Credit cards offer convenience but must be used wisely. Always pay the full bill before the due date to avoid interest charges of up to 42% per annum.

Home & Personal Loans

Loans help you achieve goals but come with EMIs. For example, a ₹5,00,000 loan at 10% for 5 years means an EMI of about ₹10,624 per month.

Emergency Fund & Savings

Always keep at least 6 months of expenses as an emergency fund. Small monthly savings of ₹2,000 can grow to ₹1,00,000+ in a few years with discipline.

Financial Tips

  • Save at least 20% of your income every month.
  • Track your expenses using a notebook or app.
  • Avoid unnecessary credit card spending.
  • Build an emergency fund of 6 months.
  • Invest early to benefit from compound interest.
  • Always read loan documents before signing.

Steps to Open a Savings Account

  1. Choose a trusted bank.
  2. Fill the account opening form.
  3. Submit ID proof & address proof.
  4. Complete KYC verification.
  5. Deposit initial amount and receive passbook.

Key Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment — fixed monthly loan payment.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The duration of a loan or investment.

Quick Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where P = Principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months.
