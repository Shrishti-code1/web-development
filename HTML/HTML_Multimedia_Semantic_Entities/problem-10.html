About the Expo

National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is India’s largest technology and career event ®, bringing together 200+ companies, 50+ speakers and 10,000+ students under one roof. The event will be held from 15–17 November 2026 at Pragati Maidan, New Delhi.

Whether you are looking for internships, placements, workshops or certifications, this expo is your gateway to a bright future. Entry is free for students with valid ID. For queries, email info@techexpo2026.in.

Organizer Message

"Our mission is to bridge the gap between talented students and leading companies. This expo is not just an event — it’s a launchpad for your career. We believe every student deserves a fair chance & the right platform."

— Dr. Ananya Verma, Expo Director & Founder

The expo is organized by TechBridge Foundation © 2026, in partnership with AICTE ® and leading universities across India.

Preparation Tips

  • Carry multiple copies of your updated resume.
  • Research the companies you want to meet.
  • Prepare a 60-second self-introduction.
  • Dress professionally and carry your college ID.
  • Keep a notebook to note important contacts.
  • Follow up with recruiters on LinkedIn after the event.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online and receive your QR pass.
  2. Collect your badge at the registration desk.
  3. Attend keynote sessions and workshops.
  4. Visit company booths and submit resumes.
  5. Attend on-spot interviews if shortlisted.
  6. Network with speakers and fellow attendees.
  7. Collect certificates and follow up with contacts.

Key Terms

Career Expo
A large event where companies meet job seekers.
Internship
A short-term work experience for students.
Placement
Direct hiring of students by companies.
Workshop
A hands-on training session on a specific skill.
Certification
An official document proving your skill or course completion.
Networking
Building professional relationships for career growth.
