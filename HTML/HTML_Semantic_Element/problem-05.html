  • Prepare elevator pitch about your skills
  • Research participating companies beforehand

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online for the event
  2. Download your entry pass
  3. Visit the venue on scheduled date
  4. Explore organization booths
  5. Attend sessions and workshops
  6. Network with recruiters
  7. Apply for opportunities
  8. Follow up after the event

Preparation Checklist

  • Carry multiple copies of your resume
  • Bring a portfolio or work samples
  • Dress professionally
  • Prepare questions for recruiters
  • Bring ID proof and entry pass
  • Carry a notepad and pen
